# Site Magic Content Checklist

Use this checklist after intake and before publishing a tenant site. Every
claim must come from submitted customer data or a verified external source.
Omit sections that lack evidence.

## Homepage

- Hero: customer-provided photo or approved media, factual headline, service
  area, primary contact action, and a secondary informational action.
- Trust bar: only verified review data, credentials, years, or job counts.
- Services: three to six priority services, each linked to a useful service
  page when enough source material exists.
- Differentiators: specific facts supplied or approved by the customer.
- Proof: verified reviews, released testimonials, and approved before/after
  media. Never fabricate proof or use protected health information.
- Story: customer-supplied origin story and approved team media.
- Local presence: verified service area, contact details, and map data.
- Lead capture: name, contact method, requested service, truthful response
  expectations, and a working submission path.
- Footer: navigation, verified contact information, social links, privacy
  link, and credentials only when public display is approved.

## Technical floor

- Mobile-first and keyboard accessible.
- Click-to-call links use the submitted phone number.
- One H1 per page and descriptive service/location titles.
- Structured data contains only public verified fields.
- Images are optimized and have meaningful alt text.
- Google Business Profile and NAP data match verified business records.
- Angel widget identifies the correct tenant and uses the first-party script.
- Missing optional data produces no placeholder claims or empty sections.

## Build order

1. Validate intake facts and customer approvals.
2. Select the design direction from real brand assets.
3. Build the homepage and priority service pages.
4. Run accessibility, governance, claim, and tenant-isolation checks.
5. Verify lead capture and Angel widget end to end.
6. Publish only after the deployment gate is approved.
