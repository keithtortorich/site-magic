## Hero Section

### Hook (H1)
**"You left money on the table this week."**

*Why this works:*
- Direct accusation (Hormozi). Makes them stop scrolling.
- True for nearly every contractor/home service business.
- Not a question, not soft. A statement.

### Subhead (H2)
**"NetBuild.Pro answers your phone so you don't lose jobs you already paid to generate."**

*Why this works:*
- No "AI." No "software." No "technology."
- Frames NetBuild.Pro as office staff, not a tool.
- Outcome-focused: "don't lose jobs" (revenue recovery).
- Connects to the problem (missed calls = lost revenue).

### Trust Bar (3 stats)
Below the subhead, before CTA:

| Stat | The Hit |
|------|---------|
| **27% of calls go unanswered** | That's revenue walking out the door |
| **85% never call back** | They call your competitor instead |
| **78% hire whoever responds first** | Speed = money |

*Why this works:*
- Math, not fluff.
- Each stat is followed by the *business implication*, not a feature.
- Reinforces the problem before offering the solution.

### CTA Button (Hero)
**"No Card. No Contract. Get a Job Or It's Free."**

*Not:* "Start Free Trial," "Sign Up," "Apply Now"

*Why this works:*
- Grand Slam Offer language (Hormozi).
- Eliminates three objections before they're raised: cost ($0), commitment (no contract), risk (if we don't deliver, it's free).
- "Get a job or it's free" is a performance guarantee. It reframes the entire offer.

### Hero CTA Secondary
**"Just Tell Me Yes."**

*Placement:* Below the main button, smaller text  
*Why:* Voss closing technique. Simple, assumes consent, hand control back to prospect.

## The Math Section

### Section Header
**"Let's Do The Math."**

### Copy Block
"How many calls do you think you're missing in a typical week?

Most contractors we talk to say 10. Sometimes more.

That's 40 a month. At $400 a job average, that's **$16,000 a month**. **$192,000 a year.**

Does that number hurt?

It should.

And that's just the jobs you *know* you're losing. There are more—the ones you never find out about because the customer already called someone else.

That's why we built NetBuild.Pro. Not to automate. To recover."

*Why this works:*
- Uses the Voss math formula: "Walk me through it → Mirroring → Labeling → The Math"
- Makes the problem *quantifiable* and *painful*.
- Transitions from problem to solution naturally.
- Ends with the core philosophy: "To recover."

## FAQ Section

### Q: "Why is the website free?"

**A (Hormozi):**
"Because we'd rather *show* you what NetBuild.Pro can do than *tell* you. Once you see it working, the rest is an easy decision."

### Q: "What happens after 14 days?"

**A (Voss):**
"You choose. We send you the details of every job we captured. Real customers, real appointments. You verify they're real. If we didn't get any, no bill. If we did, you decide whether to keep going—month-to-month, cancel anytime."

### Q: "Can I switch plans later?"

**A (Practical):**
"Yes. Most contractors start with Office Staff and move to Business Manager once they've seen the results. Some scale back if they get slower. It's your business. You're in control."

### Q: "Is there a setup fee?"

**A (Voss):**
"No. What you see is what you pay. No surprises."

### Q: "I already have someone answering the phone."

**A (Hormozi + Voss):**
"Good. NetBuild.Pro covers the hours they can't: nights, weekends, lunch breaks, when you're on a job site. It backs up your team instead of replacing them. And it never calls in sick."

### Q: "I've been burned before."

**A (Voss):**
"We're putting our money where our mouth is. No risk to you. 14 days, free. We get you a job or we cancel."

### Q: "I don't trust AI."

**A (Voss, Closing):**
"You shouldn't. That's why we don't ask you to trust us. We prove it works or we don't get paid. Fair?"

### Q: "I can't afford $497."

**A (Voss, Math):**
"Can you afford to lose $16,000 a month? Because that's what's happening right now. What's the real concern here?"

### Q: "Send me an email."

**A (Voss, Objection Handling):**
"Happy to. But real quick—what's the hesitation? Because if it works, you pay nothing. If it doesn't, you pay nothing. So what's the real objection?"

## Copy Standards Applied

| Standard | Example |
|----------|---------|
| **Never say "AI"** | "24/7 answering" not "AI answers your calls" |
| **Outcome > feature** | "Never lose a lead to voicemail" not "Automated follow-up" |
| **Math > emotion** | "$16,000 a month" not "Give yourself peace of mind" |
| **Control > urgency** | "Fair?" not "Limited time offer" |
| **Problem first** | "You left money on the table" not "WebStaffr helps contractors" |
| **Tactical language** | "How would you like to proceed?" not "Take action now" |

## Voice Checklist

- [ ] Every section answers at least one of: Capture attention, Increase desire, Remove doubt, Create action
- [ ] No corporate buzzwords (platform, solution, innovative, cutting-edge, etc.)
- [ ] No unnecessary adjectives (amazing, incredible, game-changing, etc.)
- [ ] Reading level: ~8th grade
- [ ] Each CTA has a clear, tactical next step
- [ ] Pricing section leads with outcome, not feature
- [ ] Objection handling uses Voss questions ("Fair?" "What's the hesitation?" "How would you like to proceed?")

## Tone Markers

**Hormozi (Front) = Bold, Direct, Undeniable**
- "You left money on the table this week."
- "One job pays for the month."
- "We recover revenue."

**Voss (Back) = Calm, Tactical, Control-Focused**
- "Fair?"
- "What's the hesitation?"
- "How would you like to proceed?"

*Together:* An experienced business advisor who deeply understands contractors and knows exactly how to close the deal.

## Success Criteria

After implementing this copy, measure:

1. **Click-through on primary CTA:** Should increase significantly (from current "Start Free Trial" generic button)
2. **Pricing tier selection:** Office Staff ($497) should be 70%+ of signups (vs. Site-only or Business Manager)
3. **Objection handling:** FAQ section should reduce support inquiries (if copy addresses real fears)
4. **Conversion rate:** "Get a job or it's free" offer should convert higher than risk-free trial positioning
5. **Customer feedback:** New signups should report "felt like they understood my business" (empathy indicator)
